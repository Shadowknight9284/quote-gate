    Result

    A detectable signal is not a profitable one.

    The planted signal raises the hit rate from .502 to .633 and beats the null regime on every seed, yet every trading cell loses after spread and fees: the edge is about 0.13 ticks per unit per step held, while a round trip costs about 3 ticks (1-tick spread + 2 × 1-tick fee). Source: docs/quant.md.

    Scope

    • All execution is simulated or hypothetical. No exchange account, funds, or real orders.
    • The synthetic market has a planted signal; results are not evidence of real-market profitability.
    • Freshness is time since local receipt, not the age of the data at its source.
    • Venue order records are in memory: deduplication does not survive a venue restart.
    • No claim of production readiness or exchange-grade latency.
